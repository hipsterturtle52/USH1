Regions & economy

  • Chesapeake: tobacco, servants → slaves, headrights
  • New England: Puritans, towns, fishing/ships/trade
  • Middle: diverse, tolerant, grain ("breadbasket")
  • Lower South: rice/indigo, enslaved majority (SC)
  • Mercantilism → Navigation Acts; salutary neglect → strong assemblies
  • Market revolution: Erie Canal, steamboats, Lowell mills; North industry / South cotton / West grain
  • Panics: 1819 (land, Bank), 1837 (Specie Circular), 1857, 1873
  • Reconstruction: sharecropping + crop lien = debt cycle
